Download Overlap

The latest version · Requires macOS 27

Installing

  1. Open Overlap.zip and drag Overlap into your Applications folder.
  2. Open it. macOS says it can't check Overlap for malicious software, because Overlap isn't registered with Apple. Click Done.
  3. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll to the message about Overlap, and click Open Anyway. Confirm with your password.

You only do this once. New versions install themselves: Overlap checks once a day, or choose Overlap › Check for Updates….

Comfortable with Terminal? This does steps 2 and 3 in one go, after step 1: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Overlap.app
